# IDEAS Proposal & Pitch Deck Patterns

**Last Updated: February 2026**

This reference documents the two distinct document types IDEAS uses for client engagement, derived from real proposals and pitch decks for POLRI, SID (Sekolah.mu), KAI, and Perlis.

## Document Type Classification

IDEAS produces two fundamentally different document types depending on the engagement stage and client sector.

| Attribute | Pitch Deck (Type A) | Full Proposal (Type B) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Purpose** | Secure follow-up meeting, build relationship | Close deal / secure contract |
| **Length** | 15-32 pages | 26-70+ pages |
| **Tone** | Strategic, consultative, visionary | Formal, procedural, detailed |
| **Pricing** | Lump sum, value-based, or none | Detailed breakdown (RAB/HPS) |
| **When to use** | First engagement, enterprise/BUMN, strategic discussion | Government procurement (pengadaan), RFP response |
| **Real examples** | SID, KAI, Perlis | POLRI Keseluruhan, POLRI Sebagian |

## Type A: Pitch Deck Structure

The pitch deck follows a **Problem → Approach → Outcome** narrative arc. It is designed for consultative selling — demonstrating a deep understanding of the client's challenges before presenting solutions.

### Section-by-Section Guide

| # | Section | Content | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Cover** | Project title + tagline + client logo (optional) | Co-branding with client logo for enterprise/BUMN builds rapport. |
| 2 | **Prakata** | CEO thought leadership essay by Rizki Rusdiwijaya | Standard essay: "Data adalah Aset Pendorong Inovasi dan Keunggulan yang Kompetitif". |
| 3 | **Quote** | "Good data, good decision" — keputusan terbaik lahir dari pemahaman yang utuh | Standard IDEAS quote slide. |
| 4 | **Agenda** | 4 numbered items: (1) Problem context, (2) Objectives, (3) Deliverables & Outcomes, (4) Next Steps | Always 4 items for simplicity and focus. |
| 5 | **Latar Belakang** | Section divider + client-specific pain points | Use high-quality, client-specific imagery (e.g., their office, products). |
| 6 | **Pain Points** | Visual wheel or cards showing 3-12 common data problems | Tailor visuals to the client's industry. For a bank, show compliance risk; for retail, show supply chain inefficiency. |
| 7 | **Client Context** | Client-specific research: growth story, strategic plans, annual report quotes, roadmap | Demonstrates deep homework and genuine interest in the client's success. |
| 8 | **Hidden Cost** | 3 cards: Fragmented Truth, Snowball Effect, Unclear Ownership | Adapt to client's specific challenges, linking them to tangible business losses. |
| 9 | **Our Approach** | Philosophy + strategy (e.g., "Governance that enables, not blocks") | Key themes: Shift Left, Seamless Integration, Data as a Product. |
| 10 | **Solution Steps** | 4-step approach (e.g., Workshop → Templates → Bootstrap → Enablement) | Customize steps and terminology per service type and client maturity. |
| 11 | **Outcomes** | 5 key benefits aligned with the client's stated core values or mission | Connect every outcome directly to the client's mission/vision statement. |
| 12 | **Timeline** | Weekly Gantt chart (8-24 weeks typical) | Use a clean table format with W1-Wn columns. |
| 13 | **Deliverables** | 4-5 key deliverables with icons and brief, outcome-focused descriptions | Focus on the value of the deliverable, not just the document itself. |
| 14 | **Pricing** | Single lump sum + duration in working days + scope label | Optional for purely exploratory pitches. See Pricing Models below. |
| 15 | **Company Profile** | Abbreviated: Core Values, 3 Pillars, Key Clients | Shorter than the full proposal version. See `references/company-profile.md`. |
| 16 | **Closing** | "TERIMA KASIH" + IDEAS logo | Standard, clean closing slide. |

### Sector-Specific Customization

**Enterprise (SID):** Focus on data debt, SDLC integration, tribe/squad challenges. Use tech terminology (Data Contract, PRD/BRD, Producer-Consumer). Timeline in weeks.

**BUMN (KAI):** Focus on Master Data Management, regulatory compliance (e.g., SPBE), and business process mapping. Use client imagery (trains, stations). Include multi-stream timeline per business unit.

**International Government (Perlis):** Include full company profile. Reference client's existing strategic plans and digital roadmap. Use a Discovery-Design-Build-Run methodology. No pricing is presented initially.

## Type B: Full Proposal Structure

The full proposal follows a **Scope → Plan → Cost** structure. It is designed for formal procurement processes, especially for Indonesian government clients (pengadaan).

### Section-by-Section Guide

| # | Section | Content | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Cover** | Project title + subtitle/tagline | Formal, no client logo. |
| 2 | **SMART Objectives** | Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-Bound objectives | Each letter should be expanded into 1-2 detailed sentences. |
| 3 | **Ruang Lingkup** | Detailed In-Scope (numbered list) + Out-of-Scope (numbered list) | This is a critical section for government contracts to prevent scope creep. |
| 4 | **Deliverables** | Detailed numbered list of all documents, systems, and outputs | Group by category (e.g., SOP, Standards, Reports, Dashboards). |
| 5 | **Timeline** | Visual monthly timeline (Bulan 1-N) showing phases and key milestones | Aligned with the monthly activity breakdown. |
| 6 | **Investasi: Struktur Biaya** | Detailed cost table: Personnel + Operational + Risk Management. Must be transparent. | See Pricing Model below. References `rab-hps-methodology.md`. |
| 7 | **Tim Proyek** | Team roles with detailed descriptions and responsibilities | 6-20 roles are typical, depending on scope. See Team Composition section. |
| 8 | **Aktivitas per Bulan** | For each month: Fokus Utama, Milestone, Key Activity | Provides a granular breakdown of the project plan. |
| 9 | **Company Profile** | Full version: Core Values, 3 Pillars, Services, Products, Clients, Portfolio | Appended at the end. See `references/company-profile.md`. |

### SMART Objectives Template

> **Specific:** Mengembangkan dan menyerahkan [N] Dokumen [type] yang komprehensif untuk [client], mencakup [specific elements].
> **Measurable:** Setiap dokumen divalidasi, disetujui, dan diadopsi oleh [client unit]. Keberhasilan diukur dengan [specific metric, e.g., adopsi SOP oleh 100% unit].
> **Achievable:** Tim ahli IDEAS terdiri dari [N] personil dengan peran spesifik yang berkolaborasi erat dengan tim [client] melalui workshop dan sesi kerja mingguan.
> **Relevant:** Proyek ini secara langsung menjawab kebutuhan mendesak untuk [strategic need, e.g., meningkatkan skor SPBE] dan sejalan dengan [client's strategic document].
> **Time-Bound:** Proyek akan diselesaikan dalam jangka waktu [N] bulan sejak kontrak efektif, dengan milestone yang jelas setiap bulannya.

### Monthly Activity Template

For each month (Bulan N):
- **Fokus Utama:** [One sentence describing the month's primary focus]
- **Milestone:** [2-3 key milestones to be achieved]
- **Key Activity:** [3-5 specific activities with responsible roles]

## Four Pricing Models

### Model 1: Headcount-Based (Government Proposal) — LEGACY

> **DEPRECATED**: This model uses a fixed flat rate per person per month regardless of competency. For new proposals, use **Model 4: Flexible Competency-Based** below.

Used historically for formal government procurement (pengadaan). Retained for backward compatibility with existing legacy proposals only.

| Component | Formula | Example (Legacy) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| A. Personnel | Headcount × Months × Flat_Rate | 20 × 6 × Rp 20,000,000 = Rp 2,400,000,000 |
| B. Operational | ~22.5% × A | Rp 540,400,000 |
| C. Risk Management | 10% × (A + B) | Rp 294,040,000 |
| **D. Total** | **A + B + C** | **Rp 3,234,440,000** |

> **Note:** This model is inaccurate because it applies the same rate to junior and senior staff. Use Model 4 instead.

### Model 4: Flexible Competency-Based (Government/Enterprise) — RECOMMENDED

The **recommended model** for all new proposals. Each team member is priced individually based on their **role**, **education tier** (SMK/D3/S1/S2/S3), and **years of experience**. This replaces the fixed flat rate with granular, competency-driven pricing sourced from `SALARY_MASTER` in `rate_card.py` (DataMasterSalaryRate2026.xlsx).

**Must comply with Perpres 46/2025** (replaces Perpres 16/2018) and reference **PMK 32/2025** for Standar Biaya Masukan (SBM) rates for the 2026 fiscal year.

**Government Evaluation Criteria (Perpres 46/2025):**
- **Technical Evaluation:** 60-70% weight (methodology, team competency, timeline)
- **Financial Evaluation:** 30-40% weight (reasonableness of price, completeness of components)
- **Qualification:** Company profile, past performance, certifications, tax compliance

**Competency-Based Pricing Logic:**
- **Senior/Urgent projects**: Choose team members with higher education (S2/S3) and more experience (≥8 years) → higher level → higher rate
- **Junior/Routine projects**: Choose team members with standard education (S1/D3) and fewer years (≤3 years) → lower level → lower rate
- **Education advantage**: Higher education requires fewer years of experience for the same level (e.g., S2 with 3yr = S1 with 5yr)

**Example (POLRI Keseluruhan — Flexible Model):**

- **Scaling:** For smaller scopes, reduce team size and adjust competency levels accordingly.

### Model 2 (Detail): Lump Sum with Duration (Enterprise/BUMN Pitch)

Used for enterprise and BUMN clients who prefer simplicity and value-based pricing over detailed cost breakdowns.

| Client | Price | Duration | Tax Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| SID (Sekolah.mu) | Rp 170,000,000 | ±44 hari kerja | Sudah termasuk pajak |
| KAI | Rp 575,250,000 | ±132 hari kerja | Belum termasuk pajak |

**Format:** A single, clear price card with:
- Price in a large, prominent font
- Duration in working days
- A clear scope label (e.g., "Advisory & Governance Foundation")
- Explicit tax inclusion/exclusion status

### Model 3: No Pricing (Strategic Pitch)

Used for initial, exploratory, or international pitches where the goal is to start a conversation, not close a deal. The proposal ends with the proposed methodology (e.g., Discovery-Design-Build-Run) and a call to action for a follow-up meeting to discuss scope and investment.

## Proposal Writing Best Practices (Research-Enriched)

1.  **Win Theme:** Every proposal must have a clear, client-centric win theme. Example: "Satu-satunya konsultan data di Indonesia yang telah membangun ekosistem data provinsi end-to-end dengan rekam jejak peningkatan skor SPBE."
2.  **Evidence-Based Claims:** Back all claims with quantifiable metrics from case studies (e.g., "meningkatkan skor SPBE dari 3.5 menjadi 4.1", "mengintegrasikan 3,448 dataset dari 46 OPD"). See `references/case-studies.md`.
3.  **Regulatory Alignment:** Explicitly reference applicable regulations (Perpres 39/2019, UU PDP, SPBE, Perpres 46/2025).
4.  **Risk Mitigation:** Include a 5-10% risk management buffer in all government proposals.
5.  **Dual-Language:** International proposals in English; domestic proposals in Bahasa Indonesia with technical terms in English.
6.  **PMBOK Governance:** For projects >200 man-days, include a Project Governance Framework section.
7.  **Value Focus:** Frame deliverables in terms of business value delivered, not just documents produced (PMBOK 7th Edition Principle #4: Focus on Value).
8.  **Data Value Articulation:** For advisory proposals, include a Data Value Proposition section that quantifies the economic value of the client's data using frameworks like the **Deloitte 2025 Data Valuation Framework** (Income, Cost, Market approaches) and the concept of "data debt". See `thought-leadership-frameworks.md`.

### Minto Pyramid Structure for Proposals

**Source:** Barbara Minto, *The Pyramid Principle*

A top-down communication structure for all proposals and pitch decks:

1.  **Main Message (Governing Thought):** "IDEAS is the ideal partner for [Client]'s data transformation because we offer a unique combination of proven methodology, comprehensive solutions, and a cost-effective approach."
2.  **Supporting Arguments:**
    *   **Proven Methodology:** Our 4-methodology integration (DAMA + Agile + CRISP-DM + PMBOK).
    *   **Comprehensive Solutions:** Our 15+ services and 8 proprietary product platforms.
    *   **Cost-Effective Approach:** Our transparent pricing and focus on sustainable knowledge transfer.
3.  **Evidence Layer:** Case studies (Jabar ecosystem), 14 USPs, ROI calculations, team credentials, client testimonials.

> **Principle:** Each level must answer the "Why?" or "How?" of the level above it. Each group of ideas must be MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive).

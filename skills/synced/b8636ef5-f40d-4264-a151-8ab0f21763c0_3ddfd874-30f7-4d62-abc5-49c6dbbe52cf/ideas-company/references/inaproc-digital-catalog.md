## 2. INAPROC Platform Context

### 2.1 What is INAPROC

INAPROC (Indonesia National Procurement Portal) is the centralized government procurement system managed by **LKPP** (Lembaga Kebijakan Pengadaan Barang/Jasa Pemerintah). The platform integrates four core subsystems that together form the end-to-end national procurement ecosystem.

| Subsystem | Function | URL |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **E-Katalog v6** | Marketplace — product/service catalog and e-purchasing | katalog.inaproc.id |
| **SPSE** | E-Tendering — competitive bidding and auctions | spse.inaproc.id |
| **SIKaP** | Provider database — registration and performance tracking | sikap.inaproc.id |
| **SiRUP** | Procurement planning — government procurement roadmaps | sirup.lkpp.go.id |

### 2.2 E-Katalog v6 Key Features

E-Katalog v6 was officially launched by the President of Indonesia on **10 December 2024** and became mandatory nationwide on **1 January 2025** (SE Kepala LKPP No. 9/2024). Key features include:

| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **E-Purchasing** | Direct purchase from verified catalog without tender process |
| **Mini-Kompetisi** | Competitive bidding within catalog providers (Kepka LKPP No. 93/2025) |
| **SAKTI Integration** | Auto-sync with Kemenkeu payment system via web service API |
| **SIPD Integration** | Connected to Kemendagri's regional government information system |
| **Real-time Chat** | Direct communication between provider and buyer |
| **Order Amendment** | Flexible order modification during procurement |
| **Tax Integration** | Automated tax calculation and compliance |

### 2.3 Procurement Process Flow

The e-purchasing process through INAPROC follows a streamlined digital workflow:

1. **Product Discovery** — Government agency browses katalog.inaproc.id
2. **Product Selection** — Selects product with specifications, quantity, and delivery location
3. **Order Placement** — Generates Surat Pesanan (SP) / Purchase Order
4. **Negotiation** (optional) — Real-time price negotiation via chat
5. **Contract Execution** — SPK (Surat Perintah Kontrak) generated
6. **SAKTI Sync** — Transaction data auto-syncs to SAKTI via API
7. **Payment** — SP2D (Surat Perintah Pencairan Dana) processed through SAKTI → SPAN

### 3.1 UMKK Status Advantages

IDEAS' classification as UMKK provides significant procurement advantages under Indonesian law. The government is mandated to allocate a minimum of **40% of procurement budget** to UMKK providers (Perpres 12/2021). Additional benefits include simplified registration, priority access to e-Katalog, and eligibility for LKPP capacity-building programs. UMKK providers can participate in e-Purchasing without the complex tender processes required for larger procurements.

### 7.10 Market Insights & Strategic Recommendations

The comprehensive INAPROC competitor analysis across 22 keywords reveals several actionable insights for IDEAS:

**Insight 1: Trust > Price.** Government buyers demonstrably prefer established, trusted vendors over the cheapest option. PUTRA ADI PERKASA sells Cursor at Rp 5.7M (9.5x IDEAS' price) yet has 3 sales vs IDEAS' 0. NUSANTARA TRAINING dominates with 947 sales through consistent delivery and government relationships, not lowest pricing. IDEAS should invest in building INAPROC reputation through initial transactions, reviews, and government references.

**Insight 2: Solusi Klik vulnerability.** The primary competitor's SP-2 violation (No. 30075/D.2.3/12/2025) creates a window of opportunity. Government procurement officers conducting due diligence will see the violation notice prominently displayed on Solusi Klik's profile. Violations include: Salah Kategori, Ketidaksesuaian TKDN, Pelanggaran Merek Dagang, Harga Tidak Wajar, and Kesalahan Informasi Produk.

**Insight 3: Service demand is proven.** NQA Indonesia's 14 sales for a single "Data Management" service at Rp 7.5M proves that government agencies actively procure data management services through INAPROC. NUSANTARA TRAINING's 947 sales for transformasi digital training proves massive demand for capacity building. IDEAS must list consulting services (Jasa) on e-Katalog.

**Insight 4: 7 blue oceans identified.** Data governance, data quality, data privacy, super apps, data warehouse, ML/AI, and IT strategy consulting have zero or near-zero competition on INAPROC. IDEAS can be the first mover in all 7 categories.

**Insight 5: ICONS MEDIA NUSANTARA is a media company.** The top SPBE competitor (51 sales) is primarily a PR and media monitoring company (51–200 employees) that pivoted to sell SPBE digital products. They lack deep data management and DAMA expertise. IDEAS can outcompete on domain depth.

**Insight 6: Bundling remains unique.** No competitor offers both digital tools AND consulting services on INAPROC. This dual model remains IDEAS' strongest differentiator.

**Insight 7: YAVA247 as domestic alternative.** YAVA247 Data Management Platform (Rp 168M, PDN-labeled) is distributed by Solusi Klik. As a domestic data management platform, it could be positioned as a competitor to IDEAS' platform offerings. However, IDEAS' open-source-based ecosystem and DAMA-DMBOK alignment provide deeper governance capabilities.

## 9. Glossary of Terms

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **DAMA-DMBOK** | Data Management Body of Knowledge by DAMA International. The globally recognized standard for data management. |
| **E-Katalog** | An electronic catalog system for government procurement in Indonesia, managed by LKPP. |
| **INAPROC** | Indonesia National Procurement Portal, the central hub for government procurement. |
| **KBLI** | Klasifikasi Baku Lapangan Usaha Indonesia, the Indonesian Standard Industrial Classification. |
| **LKPP** | Lembaga Kebijakan Pengadaan Barang/Jasa Pemerintah, the National Public Procurement Agency of Indonesia. |
| **PDN** | Produk Dalam Negeri, or Domestic Product. |
| **SAKTI** | Sistem Aplikasi Keuangan Tingkat Instansi, the financial application system for government agencies. |
| **SPBE** | Sistem Pemerintahan Berbasis Elektronik, the Indonesian government's e-government system. |
| **SPK** | Surat Perintah Kontrak, or Contract Award Letter. |
| **TKDN** | Tingkat Komponen Dalam Negeri, the local content requirement for goods/services procured by the government. |
| **UMKK** | Usaha Mikro, Kecil, dan Koperasi, referring to Micro, Small, and Cooperative enterprises. |

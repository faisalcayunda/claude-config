# IDEAS Services Catalog

**Last Updated: February 2026**

## Service Pillars Overview

IDEAS structures its **14 core services** into three primary pillars, meticulously designed to guide clients through every phase of their data transformation journey—from foundational strategy to implementation and long-term sustainability. Each service is delivered with a **Product Thinking** mindset, treating every deliverable as a distinct product with clearly defined users, business value, and a lifecycle. This approach ensures that our solutions are not just technically sound but are also valuable, usable, and integrated into the client's operational fabric.

| Pilar | Fokus | Target Audiens | Lifecycle Stage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Advisory & Governance** | Building a robust strategic foundation and a clear directional path. | C-Level, Data/IT Heads | Ideation → Planning |
| **2. Platform & Solution Implementation** | Translating strategy into tangible, operational systems and solutions. | CTO, CIO, CPO | Development → Launch |
| **3. Empowerment & Managed Services** | Ensuring adoption, sustainability, and organizational self-sufficiency. | DGO, HR, IT Ops | Growth → Maturity |

> **Complementary Channel — INAPROC E-Katalog:** Beyond our 14 core consulting services, IDEAS operates a **digital product distribution business line** via the INAPROC e-Katalog v6, featuring 24 international SaaS products (including AI tools, design software, and analytics platforms). This allows government agencies to procure tools directly through e-Purchasing, streamlining the acquisition process. These products complement our consulting services; for instance, a client undergoing an SPBE Assessment (S1.1) can directly purchase Figma for UI/UX design, or a DGO client (S3.1) can procure ClickUp for task management. For a complete list, see `inaproc-digital-catalog.md`.

## Pillar 1: Advisory & Governance

Services in this pillar are dedicated to translating business ambitions into measurable data strategies and establishing trustworthy governance frameworks. We integrate global standards like **DAMA-DMBOK** (including awareness of the ongoing **DMBOK 3.0 project** with its focus on AI, cloud, and modern platforms) with the specific context of Indonesian regulations (e.g., UU PDP, SPBE, Satu Data Indonesia) to build a solid foundation for our clients.

### S1.4 Data Asset Valuation & Monetization Advisory

Helps organizations understand, measure, and communicate the economic value of their data assets using established frameworks like Infonomics and the **Deloitte 2025 Data Valuation methodology**. This service bridges the gap between viewing data as an operational cost and recognizing it as a strategic asset with measurable economic value. We incorporate the concept of **"data debt"** (lifecycle costs, quality issues, compliance risks) to provide a balanced valuation.

| Aspek | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **DAMA KAs** | KA-01, KA-10 |
| **Lifecycle Stage** | Planning, Assessment, Strategy |
| **Methodology** | Infonomics 6-Model Framework, COPQ Analysis, **Deloitte 2025 (Income, Cost, Market Approaches)** |
| **Must Deliverables** | Data Asset Inventory, Data Valuation Report (IVI/BVI/CVI), COPQ Analysis Report, Data Value Communication Deck, Data Monetization Opportunity Assessment |
| **Should Deliverables** | Financial Valuation Report (MVI/EVI), Data Value Dashboard, Balance Sheet Readiness Assessment, Data Marketplace Feasibility Study, **Data Debt Report** |
| **Est. Total Effort** | 35-55 man-days |
| **Deliverable Map** | See `service-deliverables-map.md` → S1.4 |

**Value Proposition:** Transforms the perception of data from a "cost center" to a "value center" with quantifiable economic proof. It provides a quantitative basis for data management investments, supports governance business cases, and unlocks data monetization opportunities.

**Service Tiers:**

| Paket | Deskripsi | Target Klien |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Value Discovery** | An initial data value assessment using foundational models (IVI, BVI) plus COPQ analysis. | Organizations wanting to understand the baseline value of their data and build a business case for data management investment. |
| **Comprehensive Valuation** | A complete valuation using all 6 Infonomics models and financial approaches (Income, Cost, Market). | Organizations requiring a formal valuation for M&A, investor reporting, or strategic planning. |
| **Monetization Strategy** | The Comprehensive Valuation plus a data monetization strategy and marketplace feasibility study. | Organizations aiming to generate direct revenue from their data assets. |

**Regulatory Context:** This service considers global regulatory trends regarding the recognition of data as a balance sheet asset, such as China's "Interim Provisions" (2024), the evolution of IAS 38/IFRS for intangible assets, and the Indonesian context (Satu Data Indonesia, UU PDP No. 27/2022).

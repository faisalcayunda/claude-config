# IDEAS Company Role Definitions (v2.0)

This document provides a comprehensive overview of all **141 roles** within PT Indonesian Data Ecosystem (IDEAS), detailing their responsibilities, required skills, and career pathways. The definitions are modeled after the UK Government's Digital, Data and Technology (DDaT) Capability Framework to ensure alignment with global best practices.

## Role Structure

Each role is defined with the following structure:

- **Role Title:** The official job title.
- **Group:** The organizational group the role belongs to (e.g., Board, Biz&Digital, PDT, DAMA).
- **Category:** The salary and competency category (e.g., Management, Managerial, Lead, Analyst, Engineer, Officer).
- **Role Summary:** A brief, one-paragraph overview of the role's purpose and function.
- **Key Responsibilities:** A bulleted list of the primary duties and accountabilities.
- **Skills & Competencies:** A table outlining the required skills, proficiency levels, and their descriptions, adapted from the DDaT framework.

## AI Ethics Officer

| Group | Category |
|---|---|
| PDT | Analyst |

### Role Summary

The AI Ethics Officer is responsible for guiding the ethical development and deployment of artificial intelligence solutions for our clients. This role involves establishing and championing a framework for AI ethics and governance, ensuring that AI systems are fair, transparent, accountable, and aligned with both Indonesian societal values and international best practices. The AI Ethics Officer will work collaboratively with data scientists, engineers, and consultants to embed ethical considerations throughout the entire project lifecycle, from data acquisition to model deployment, thereby upholding the company's commitment to responsible innovation in the data and AI landscape.

### Key Responsibilities

*   Develop and implement a comprehensive AI ethics framework, including policies, guidelines, and best practices tailored to the Indonesian context.
*   Conduct ethical risk assessments and impact analyses for all AI projects to identify and mitigate potential harms, biases, and unintended consequences.
*   Advise and train project teams on the principles of responsible AI, including fairness, transparency, accountability, and privacy.
*   Serve as the primary point of contact for clients and internal stakeholders on all matters related to AI ethics and responsible innovation.
*   Monitor and audit AI systems to ensure ongoing compliance with ethical standards and regulatory requirements.
*   Stay abreast of emerging trends, research, and regulations in AI ethics and communicate their implications to the organization.
*   Facilitate discussions and workshops to foster a culture of ethical awareness and critical thinking across the organization.
*   Collaborate with legal and compliance teams to ensure that AI practices adhere to all relevant laws and regulations.

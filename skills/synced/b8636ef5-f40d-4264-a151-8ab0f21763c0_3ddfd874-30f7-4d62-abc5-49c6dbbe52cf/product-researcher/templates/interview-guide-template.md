## Introduction (5 min)
"Thank you for joining. I'm [name] from [company]. We're trying to understand [topic]. There are no right or wrong answers — I'm here to learn from your experience. This session will take about [X] minutes. May I record for notes?"
## Warm-Up (5 min)
- "Tell me about your role and what a typical day/week looks like."
- "How long have you been doing [relevant activity]?"
## Core Questions — Past Behavior (20-30 min)
### Topic 1: [Problem/Need Area]
- "Tell me about the last time you [relevant activity]..." (Mom Test: specifics, not opinions)
- "Walk me through what happened step by step."
- "What was the hardest part?" (Follow-up: "Why was that hard?")
- "How did you try to solve that?" (Current alternatives)
### Topic 2: [Second Area]
- "Tell me about a time when [scenario]..."
- "What did you do next?"
### JTBD Questions (if applicable)
- "What was going on in your life that made you look for a solution?" (Push)
- "What did you hope [product] would do for you?" (Pull)
- "What concerns did you have about switching?" (Anxiety)
- "What kept you using [old solution] for so long?" (Habit)
## Reflection (5 min)
- "Of everything we discussed, what's the biggest challenge for you?"
- "Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?"
## Wrap-Up (2 min)
"Thank you so much. [Mention next steps / incentive]. Would you be open to a follow-up?"

## Presentation Tips (Knaflic + Duarte)
- Lead with the "so what?" — don't make the audience wait
- One insight per slide — not one chart per slide
- Annotate charts directly — don't make people read legends
- Use the "3-second rule" — the point should be clear in 3 seconds

## Coaching Stance — "Ship Value, Not Features"

- **Ask "What outcome are we optimizing for?"** — "Velocity is a planning tool, not a performance metric. Forsgren's research proves high performers optimize for DORA metrics — deployment frequency and lead time — not story points per sprint."
- **Push back on big-batch releases** — "Humble & Farley's Continuous Delivery principle: if releasing is painful, do it more often. Feature flags (Hodgson) let you decouple deployment from release. Ship small, learn fast."
- **Champion flow over utilization** — "Reinertsen's First Principle: Manage queues, not utilization. 100% utilization = infinite queue time (Little's Law). Vacanti shows WIP limits are the single most effective intervention."
- **Bridge delivery and GTM** — "Great engineering means nothing if customers don't adopt. Dunford's positioning + Bush's PLG + Moore's Crossing the Chasm = the GTM trifecta. Ship AND land."

## Content Enrichment Framework

### Deliverable Quality Principles
- **Flow-First Thinking** (Reinertsen): Optimize for flow (reduce WIP, minimize batch size, manage queues) before scaling processes.
- **SCQA** for launch plans and change management: Situation (market context) → Complication (competitive pressure/urgency) → Question (how do we win?) → Answer (GTM strategy).
- **Data Storytelling** (Knaflic): DORA dashboards and growth metrics use assertion-evidence charts with trend analysis.
- **Cost of Delay** (Reinertsen): Prioritize using economic value of time; WSJF = Cost of Delay ÷ Job Duration.
